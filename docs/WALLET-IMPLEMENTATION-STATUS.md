## Implemented behavior

1. One company Monnify source backs exact integer-kobo records. Provider available cash is reduced by remaining funded envelopes, all unsettled staff entitlements, active vendor principal and company fee reserves. Serialized funding transactions prevent concurrent local commitments from promising the same capacity.
2. Subsidiary funded budgets support creation, parent subdivision, increase, reduction, reallocation and closure. Parent subdivision transfers remaining capacity rather than reserving it twice. Reductions cannot consume staff entitlements or pending transfers. Historical planning budgets remain distinct from funded envelopes.
3. GMD approval alone releases funds. Current account status, configured financial designation, exact server proposal hash, unchanged source and a recent password challenge are checked inside execution. A role label, model message, delegation token or callback cannot approve money.
4. Staff requests retain salary/project/reimbursement/allowance categories and source attribution. Whole payroll batches credit exact reviewed net salaries, including multi-subsidiary allocations. Gross costs and statutory obligations are not presented as employee wallet cash. Source uniqueness prevents repeat regular salary releases.
5. Vendor invoices use a separate direct-payment flow, as confirmed by the owner. Verified supplier destination, invoice and accepted goods-receipt checks form the proposal. GMD approval reserves the budget and queues that exact destination through Monnify. The staff requester receives no wallet credit. Definitively failed or reversed vendor transfers require a new GMD approval to retry.
6. Staff can see only their own wallet and withdraw their own GMD-approved available credits without a second business approval. Multiple source lots may fund one withdrawal while retaining attribution. Current-password proof binds the session, source amounts, bank version and idempotency key. Bank enquiry uses supported bank codes and requires a matching approved staff name. An enquiry is not independent identity verification.
7. Company fees are reserved before release. Configured minimum withdrawal and maximum fee determine conservative future fee exposure. Actual provider fees are separately journaled; they are not deducted from staff principal.
8. Durable withdrawal holds and worker leases precede submission. Submission is marked before the network call. Lost responses lead to querying the same merchant reference, never blindly submitting a new reference. Unknown/pending transfers keep their holds. Success, confirmed failure and reversal have separate balanced, idempotent journal effects.
9. Signed callbacks enter a deduplicated inbox and wake authenticated transaction queries. Callback bodies alone cannot settle money. Balance observations are immutable; deficits, mismatches, unexpected transfers and unresolved callbacks block new commitments. Pausing does not stop reconciliation of existing transfers.
10. Reconciliation scans provider history after an explicit cutover timestamp, identifies unmatched references, queues known transfers for authenticated checks and refreshes backing. A page-limited scan reports that it is incomplete. Selected corrected configuration/fee issues can be acknowledged by GMD with a recorded note and fresh backing; activation remains a separate strong-authentication action. Monetary mismatches cannot be waived this way.
11. Staff may cancel a never-submitted hold or voluntarily return unused project/request credits. Salary and reimbursement cannot be clawed back through that return action. Accountability distinguishes wallet credit, bank settlement, held funds and unused returns. Source history and posted journals are protected by database constraints and immutable-history triggers.
12. AI is exclusively for GMD. Staff, HR and Super Admin are denied AI routes and delegation. GMD AI can inspect permitted wallet information, stage exact releases and budget changes, and display backend-verified approval cards. Passwords stay in a secure confirmation form, outside AI messages. No AI tool possesses a general provider transfer or approval capability.
13. Browser and both native applications expose the appropriate wallet/funding screens. GMD mobile includes request/payroll previews, vendor bank verification, budget controls, provider history reconciliation, transfer status, pause/activation and restricted issue acknowledgment. Staff mobile persists pending withdrawal identity securely to prevent a network retry from creating a new intent.
14. Wallet mode blocks legacy payment, salary sign-off, budget and emergency shortcuts that could bypass the funded-release service. Existing paid history is retained. Administrative bank/profile/password paths cannot silently take over a funded wallet owner's withdrawal identity. Approved leave retains ordinary portal access; suspended/terminated accounts do not.

## Activation boundary

Wallet mode and live transfers are off by default. No production migration, Monnify account change, provider payment, n8n deployment or app publication has been performed. Source completion is separate from operational acceptance. Follow [the rollout runbook](MONNIFY-WALLET-ROLLOUT.md).

Before real money: merchant sandbox contract tests, callback verification, fee ceiling, static egress/provider authorization settings, opening-source reconciliation, scheduler deployment, credential configuration and rotation, native device acceptance and an agreed controlled pilot remain required. The old n8n guide contained an embedded automation credential; the guide and generator now contain no credential. Any previously distributed credential must be rotated in its owning service before rollout.

Unresolved business policy is recorded explicitly: secure offboarding settlement, bank-name mismatch recovery, GMD succession, optional additional OTP/PIN/cooling-off and period carry-forward. Current configurable safeguards are NGN, ₦100 minimum, ₦25 million principal and 10 withdrawals per rolling 24 hours, whole-batch payroll, strict name match, current-password transaction proof, and provider-set maximum company fee. These are implementation defaults, not additional owner-confirmed policies.

The wallet interfaces show the latest 100 source credits/transfers where labelled; aggregate balances use the full ledger. Raw source data remains retained in the database. No provider balance read can prevent an unrelated merchant-console payout between observation and submission: restrict other source-account spending and reconcile continuously.

Use forward migrations, not `prisma db push`, which would bypass custom SQL safeguards. Do not disable the feature and resume legacy payments for sources already released to wallets. Preserve their liabilities, holds, references and reconciliation until resolved.
